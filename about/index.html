Frequently asked questions

What is a fractional product analytics consultant?

In product analytics, we're particularly obsessed with experimentation — running proper A/B tests instead of eyeballing a chart and guessing whether a feature caused an uptick. It's the most scientific way to know whether what you shipped actually worked, especially once a product gets complex enough that isolating one change's impact stops being simple. A fractional product analytics consultant brings that discipline — metrics, tracking, and experimentation — to your team without the cost or commitment of a full-time hire.

How is this different from hiring a full-time analyst?

You get senior-level expertise on the schedule your business actually needs — from a fixed-scope project, to a 2–3 day/week fractional engagement, to full-time embedded support for a set period — and you get answers now, not six months from now.

Who is this for?

Product teams at three stages: small teams (1–10 people or Pre-Seed/Seed) that need a clear go/no-go on a problem; medium teams (10–50 or Series A/B) with several stakeholders to align; and larger organizations (50+ or Series C and beyond) that need a senior product analyst embedded for a period.

How does pricing work?

Small teams can start with a fixed-scope offer. Medium and larger teams usually work with me on a fractional (2–3 days/week, 3-month minimum) or embedded basis. After a discovery call, I send a tailored proposal within 48 hours with clear deliverables, timelines, and pricing.

What does an engagement look like from start to finish?

Three steps: a discovery call to understand your goals, a tailored proposal within 48 hours, and collaborative delivery with regular updates and feedback built in along the way.

Where are you based, and do you work with international clients?

Based in Berlin, Germany, and available to work with clients globally.

What kind of companies have you worked with?

12+ years across companies including Bolt, Delivery Hero, King (Candy Crush), and EyeEm — spanning ride-hailing, food delivery, gaming, and B2B/B2C product analytics.

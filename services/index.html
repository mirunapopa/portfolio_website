What a longer engagement can look like

Every plan is scoped to where you're starting from — here's one shape a 12-week engagement often takes.

Weeks 1–2

Audit

Instrumentation, data quality, existing dashboards and business questions.

Weeks 3–6

Foundations

North Star and product KPI framework, better tracking and reporting.

Weeks 7–10

Analysis

Cohort, retention and funnel analysis, plus an experimentation framework.

Weeks 11–12

Handoff

Train PMs and the data team, and leave behind a sustainable analytics roadmap.
